What you see
Arctic sea ice reaches its annual minimum in September after the summer melt season. This chart plots that September minimum each year from 1979 (when continuous satellite records began) to 2024. Each dot is one year; the color groups it by decade.

How to interact
Click any point to see the full 12-month seasonal cycle for that year compared to the 1980s average. Toggle the trend line to see the long-term decline. Use the decade buttons to isolate periods. Press Play to animate.

Source
National Snow and Ice Data Center (NSIDC), Sea Ice Index v3.0. NASA Earth Science.
